## Security

- Disconnect a Zakura header sync peer whose headers fail contextual
  difficulty or time validation, and never acknowledge the failed headers.
  State's rejection of such a branch is now attributed to the peer instead of
  being logged as a local `state_error`. Local failures, such as a pruned
  parent or a stale preparation, keep the connection
  ([#1475](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1475)).
