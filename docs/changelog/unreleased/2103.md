## Changed

- Relayed blocks are now advertised in measured round-trip-time order, like
  locally mined blocks, so the fastest peers hear first and are the likeliest to
  relay onward. Every fourth advertisement still goes to a random remaining peer
  ([#2103](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2103)).
- Peers that announced a block to us are no longer advertised that block. They
  already have it, so they never requested it, and their advertisements held an
  advert-window entry until the block request wait expired. The new
  `peer.block_gossip.advert_skipped` counter reports the skips
  ([#2103](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2103)).
