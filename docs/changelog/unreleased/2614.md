## Fixed

- With `[network.block_download_score_and_hedge]` enabled, a slow download of a large block no
  longer makes the peer that delivered it look slow. A block reply now counts toward a legacy
  peer's expected answer time only when it is at most 14,600 serialized bytes, small enough to
  arrive in one round trip like a ping. Before, a multi-second download of a 1 MB block could
  raise that peer's expected time several times over, and the next block's single second request
  went early to a far peer instead of waiting for a nearby one. Larger replies still clear a
  missed-block mark, and are written to `delivery_score.jsonl` as `delivery_sample` rows with
  `kind: "large_block"` and an unchanged score. Block rows of both kinds now carry `wire_bytes`,
  the serialized size of the reply's blocks
  ([#2614](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2614)).
