## Fixed

- `zakurad` utility commands that fail, such as `prune-state`, `rollback-state`
  and `verify-historical-treestates`, now write their final log lines before
  exiting with status 1. `zakurad` no longer depends on the Abscissa
  application framework; its command line, configuration, output and exit
  statuses are otherwise unchanged
  ([#1419](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1419)).
