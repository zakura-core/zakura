## Changed

- Followed Zakura Common in making the post-quantum account signature scheme
  pluggable: the node's account module and helpers are renamed from `wots` to
  `pq`, the account sigop charge comes from Common's account check, the regtest
  setting is renamed `experimental_pq_transparent_activation_height`, and the
  regtest demo moves to `tools/pq-regtest`. Zakura Common is repinned to the
  commit that adds the scheme abstraction
  ([#1778](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1778)).
