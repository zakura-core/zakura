## Changed

- Legacy peers that do not advertise `NODE_NETWORK`, such as pruned nodes, are
  now kept in the address book and gossiped with their real services, within a
  bounded share of each `addr` response. They are still never dialed, and are
  no longer written to the peer cache, which is dialed at startup
  ([#1183](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1183)).
