## Changed

- Bounded native P2P raw frames, queued frames without another owner, and
  request-stream workers across each connection and the node. A saturated bound
  now pauses the affected stream's reader or sender instead of retaining more
  memory, and each stream keeps room for one frame. Connection teardown now
  waits for its aborted stream workers before returning its connection slot
  ([#1620](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1620)).
