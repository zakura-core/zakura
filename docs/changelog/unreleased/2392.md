## Changed

- On an equal-work tie, a block that was announced first now keeps its place
  when its body arrives within 2 seconds, so a full block no longer loses to an
  empty block announced later. A body that arrives after the window is ranked
  by arrival, as before. The `sync.block.announcement_claim` counter and the
  `sync.block.announcement_claim.age.seconds` histogram record the outcomes
  ([#2392](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2392)).
