Block interval

Seconds between header times, with the rolling 102-block median and the target spacing.

Difficulty

Difficulty (log) and its per-block change. The rule clamps the timespan it adjusts by, not the step itself, so single steps stay within about −24% / +19%.

Difficulty: live vs expected

Live difficulty against what the rule requires from the stored headers (should overlay exactly), the projection frozen before activation at a constant hashrate (expected = Monte Carlo median, with its 10–90% band) and the projection re-run from the current chain and hashrate. The dashed level is where difficulty settles at today's hashrate.

Block time: observed vs expected

Rolling 102-block mean and median of the observed intervals, the time each block was expected to take at its difficulty and the hashrate of the previous 120 blocks, and the projections' expected block time ahead of the tip.

Recent block times

Each block's interval at its header time, with the block time the model expected for it.

Miners

Blocks per miner per bucket, from coinbase tags and payout addresses. Shielded payouts without a tag share one bucket.

NSM balance

Balance after each block and its per-block change next to the fee share plus reissuance the block should add.

Fee split

Aggregate fees per block, split 40% miner / 60% NSM after activation. Wider bars merge blocks that landed between scrapes.

Block fullness

Orchard actions against the 330 per-block limit, transactions, and size.

Transaction fees

Mempool fee quantiles and the node's per-transaction fee quantiles for mined transactions, in zatoshi.

Mempool and peers

Mempool transactions and connected peers.

Node latency

Block verification and state commit times (ms), one-minute quantiles.
