## Changed

- Sapling bundle verification is now memoized, so a transaction's Sapling
  proofs and signatures are not verified a second time when the block that
  mines it arrives. This extends the Orchard and Ironwood memo added in #597,
  and covers roughly 12% more of mainnet's shielded verification work. The
  memo metrics move from `zakura.consensus.halo2.memo.*` to
  `zakura.consensus.memo.*` with a `verifier` label, which also gives each
  Orchard circuit era its own hit rate
  ([#600](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/600)).
