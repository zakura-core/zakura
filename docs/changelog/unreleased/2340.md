## Fixed

- Count inbound TLS attempts and control handshakes against one pending budget,
  so outbound dials cannot exceed the limit while inbound TLS attempts remain
  pending ([#2340](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2340)).
- Limit the entire inbound v2 control handshake to ten seconds, so peers cannot
  extend the connection hold by pacing successive control steps
  ([#2340](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2340)).
- Close v2 connections neutrally when their handshake captures an obsolete
  capability generation, so an in-flight handshake cannot miss header-sync
  renegotiation ([#2340](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2340)).
- Limit failed inbound v2 handshake attempts per address with a refundable
  cadence derived from the connection limit and dialer backoff: sixteen tokens
  and sixteen tokens per second by default. Empty buckets use neutral Retry
  or Refuse responses ([#2340](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2340)).
