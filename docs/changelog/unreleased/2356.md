## Changed

- `getblocktemplate` no longer waits for the mempool on a new tip when the
  wait is not expected to pay off. The mempool now reports how much work a
  new block is before it removes that block's transactions, and a long poll
  answered on the new tip returns the coinbase-only template at once when
  the mempool held no transactions or reports more work than it is expected
  to finish within the 10 ms wait, without sending the mempool a request.
  Otherwise it waits as before. The `mempool_tip_processed` trace rows record
  the reported work in its parts (`block_parts`, `mempool_parts`, `work`) and
  `ready_us`, the time from that report until the block's transactions are
  published; `gbt_empty_returned` rows record why the answer was
  coinbase-only ([#2356](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2356)).
