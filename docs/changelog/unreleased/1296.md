## Security

- Bound native QUIC connection state through handshake failures, transport
  drain, and retained stream cleanup. Preserve outbound capacity when inbound
  connections or control handshakes fill their share. Keep failed and closed
  inbound attempts charged to their source IP while their state may remain, and
  hold failed and closed connections in a separate draining budget, so failed
  handshakes stay out of the inbound share until that budget is full. Allow a bounded reconnect attempt
  from an IP at its limit so the peer can clear a stale connection. Dials
  refused by local capacity no longer count as peer failures or make discovery
  redial in a loop
  ([#1296](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1296)).
