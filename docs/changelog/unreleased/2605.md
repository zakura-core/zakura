## Changed

- Ranked an equal-work block whose header native header sync admitted first
  ahead of later blocks when its body follows within 2 seconds, regardless
  of block size. A full block whose body
  arrives within that deadline therefore no longer loses to an empty block
  announced later. A body that arrives after the deadline is ranked by arrival,
  as before, and a node does not abandon a block it mined for a rival whose
  header came first. The `sync.block.announcement_claim` counter and the
  `sync.block.announcement_claim.age.seconds` histogram record the outcomes by
  block size, and `sync.block.announcement_claim.refused` counts claims refused
  by a full table ([#2605](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2605)).
- Changed `zakura_consensus::router::init_with_read_state` to take the
  `AnnouncementClaims` table that header sync writes to and a subscription
  to the committed non-finalized chains
  ([#2605](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2605)).
