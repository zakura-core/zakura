This PR adds `discovery/late-response-admitted`, its saved Plan and its
planted case to the simulator's `discovery/wire` harness. It also lets
`cargo xtask planted` and `zakura-sim record --also-violates` accept a
case's `also_violates` IDs on the committed input. The simulator and xtask
aren't published, and release builds don't change.
