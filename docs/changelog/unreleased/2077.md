## Fixed

- Relayed blocks of every size are now advertised to every peer at once, instead of in 3 s waves
  for blocks over `advert_window_small_size_bytes`. Uploads of those larger blocks now wait for
  one of the shared upload slots, for at most 5 s, so a burst of requests cannot start every
  upload at once ([#2077](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2077)).
