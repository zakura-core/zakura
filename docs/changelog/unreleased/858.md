## Added

- ZIP 235 "Network Sustainability Mechanism: Burning of Transaction Fees", behind
  the off-by-default `zip235` build feature: from NU7 activation onwards, a block's
  coinbase must remove at least 60% of the block's transaction fees from
  circulation through the ZIP 233 `zip233Amount` field. Block templates burn exactly
  that minimum and pay the miner the remaining 40%. The feature enables `zip233`,
  and so needs the same `RUSTFLAGS='--cfg zcash_unstable="nu7"'`
  ([#858](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/858)).
