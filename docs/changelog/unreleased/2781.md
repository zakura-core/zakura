## Changed

- The sync lookahead limits now scale by exactly `75 / spacing`, rounded up,
  so they keep their time window at NU7.1's 20 second spacing instead of
  shrinking by a fifth. Near the final checkpoint, the state keeps queued
  checkpoint outputs for that wider lookahead too, so a block just past the
  final checkpoint doesn't time out waiting for one. Today's 75 and 25 second
  spacings are unchanged. NU7.1 has no Mainnet or Testnet activation height
  ([#2781](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2781)).
