## Changed

- `getblocktemplate` no longer waits for the mempool on a new tip when the
  wait is not expected to pay off. The mempool now reports how much work a
  new block is before it removes that block's transactions, and keeps what it
  holds published as it changes. A long poll that wakes on a new tip
  estimates the block's work from what the mempool holds and the block's
  transaction count, and returns the coinbase-only template at once when the
  mempool holds nothing or the work is more than it is expected to finish
  within the wait, without waiting for the mempool's report or sending it a
  request. Otherwise it waits at most
  5 ms (was 10 ms), and answers coinbase-only if it only gets to run after
  that. The `mempool_tip_processed` trace rows record the reported work in its
  parts (`block_parts`, `mempool_parts`, `work`), `ready_us`, the time from
  that report until the block's transactions are published, and `drained`,
  the finished downloads stored in that time;
  `gbt_empty_returned` rows record why the answer was coinbase-only
  ([#2356](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2356)).
