## Security

- Bound legacy P2P response framing and decoded gossip. A responder stops at 8,192 frames per response, a requester accepts only the frames its responder's chunks need, and decoded gossip takes at most 8 places in the queue it shares with requests, no more than 2 from one peer ([#1564](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1564)).

## Changed

- Bump `zakura-network` to 20.0.0 for the new `LegacyGossipError::ResponseFrameBudget` variant. Consumers with exhaustive `LegacyGossipError` matches must handle it ([#1564](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1564)).
