## Changed

- When the peer of an outbound connection that was open for 10 minutes closes
  it, Zakura now dials the peer again about 10, 30, 60 and 120 seconds later,
  until one dial succeeds. Each peer's times are scaled at random by up to a
  quarter, so the nodes that a restarted peer was connected to do not all
  redial it at once. This helps a node reconnect to a peer that restarted
  before other nodes take its free inbound slots, but it does not guarantee
  it. Each redial uses an outbound connection slot, and a banned peer, a peer
  that is already connected again, and a peer that another dial is already
  attempting are not redialed. The `peer.outbound.redials` metrics count the
  redials that are scheduled, attempted, and reconnected
  ([#2398](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2398)).
