DATA & DEFINITIONS

One node. A live view.

This dashboard observes a Zakura node on Zcash mainnet. It is a separate service with its own history. It does not depend on the block processing profiler.

Freshness
Chain data is collected every 5 seconds, metrics every 15 seconds, and peers and host data every 30 seconds. Failed or overdue sources are marked stale. Pausing only freezes your view.
History
Up to 24 hours of real samples are retained. Time ranges apply to the three activity charts. Gaps mean missing samples. History starts when this service starts collecting.
Block pipeline
These are current queue counts and exporter latency summaries. They are not a trace of any individual block. Zero work in flight is normal while a synced node waits for a block.
Headers and finalized storage
Headers are the best eligible header chain, not a promise that the blocks are valid. Finalized storage is Zakura’s storage boundary, not irreversible proof-of-work finality.
Latency
p50 is the median. p95 is the value below which 95% of observations fall. Latencies use the exporter’s rolling window, independent of the chart range. Missing values are shown as —.
Traffic and peers
Traffic covers the legacy transport. Native connections are shown separately. RPC peer counts and transport gauges may overlap. These are local observations, not a map of every Zcash node.
Privacy
Only selected aggregate metrics and public chain fields are served. Peer addresses, node logs, credentials, and wallet data are not published.
