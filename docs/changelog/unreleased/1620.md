## Changed

- Bounded native P2P raw frames and queued frames without another owner across
  each connection and the node. A connection whose local consumers lag behind
  its budget now pauses that stream's reader or sender instead of retaining more
  memory, and each stream keeps room for one frame. The node budgets hold every
  connection's budget, so one peer's traffic cannot pause another. Connection
  teardown now waits for its aborted stream workers before returning its
  connection slot
  ([#1620](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1620)).
