## Changed

- Native P2P now checks its combined resource limits at startup and refuses an
  overflowing bound or an invalid decoded allowance. A new counter reports owner
  counts above their bound
  ([#1884](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1884)).
- The node-wide serving output budget now holds every serving session's budget,
  so conformant peers can no longer pause each other's block and header serving.
  At the default peer limits, block sync and header sync can each reserve 32 GiB of
  unsent responses. The new `[network.zakura.block_sync] node_serve_output_bytes`
  and `[network.zakura.header_sync] node_serve_output_bytes` settings lower that
  budget ([#1884](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1884)).
