## Added

- Added opt-in peer distribution and verified caching of release-pinned spentness artifacts
  ([#961](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/961)).

## Fixed

- Validate spentness metadata before importing release artifacts. Restore interrupted
  imports before accepting another bundle.
- Retry artifact acquisition across peer cohorts. Distinguish busy peers, absent
  artifacts, response-capacity limits, and out-of-range requests without penalizing peers.
- Retry a busy source after a short pause instead of dropping it. Skip sources that lack
  the artifact without spending a source attempt.
- Bound artifact serving at 8 MiB/s per node and 2 MiB/s per peer, without delaying
  replies on an idle node.
- Keep at most three partial downloads per artifact, and delete stale partial files.
- Never download, serve, or advertise a revoked artifact.
- Reject replay-cache output aliases and recheck archive boundaries after artifact scans.
