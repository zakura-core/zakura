## Changed

- The `tracing.filter` setting now accepts only `level`, `target` and
  `target=level` directives. `zakurad` refuses to start if the filter contains
  a span or field directive such as `[span{field=value}]=trace`, or any
  directive it cannot parse, instead of printing a warning and ignoring it
  ([#1634](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1634)).
