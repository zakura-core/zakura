## Changed

- With BPv1 enabled, when native block sync commits a block that BPv1 is still collecting, BPv1 rebuilds the block's parts from the committed body and serves all of them to peers, instead of holding the half-collected block until its 15 s deadline. The new `zakura.p2p.bpv1.rebuild` counter records rebuilds and mismatches ([#2799](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2799)).
