## Added

- `zakurad` exports NU7 chain metrics: the best chain tip's header time, block
  interval, difficulty, target spacing and averaging window, value pools, NSM
  balance and reorgs (`zcash.chain.tip.*`, `zcash.chain.value_pool.*`,
  `zcash.chain.nsm.*`, `zcash.chain.reorg.*`), and each verified block's fees
  with their miner/NSM split, subsidy, reissuance bonus and operation counts
  (`zcash.chain.block.*`)
  ([#2017](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2017)).
