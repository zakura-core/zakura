This PR only restructures the header-sync reactor's internals. Storage
completions become a typed enum, locator reads gain exact keys, and the
reactor's scheduling records move into a `ReactorState` value. Pure transitions
now decide offers, pages, and repair suppliers. Retained headers keep their
capacity charged through prepare, apply, refusal, shutdown, and cancellation.
One new counter, `sync.header.port.unknown_settlement`, counts a storage call
that settles twice or never started. Wire formats, configuration, and traces do
not change, so it has no operator- or crate-consumer-visible effect.
