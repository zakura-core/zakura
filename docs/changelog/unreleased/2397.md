## Changed

- Zakura now keeps an inbound connection slot reserved for a peer that has
  just closed its connection, so a node that restarts is more likely to
  reconnect to its peers. A peer whose connection was open for 10 minutes
  keeps its slot for its IP address for 3 minutes after it closes the
  connection. A connection that Zakura closes itself keeps no slot. At most a
  tenth of the inbound connection limit is held this way, and new peers are
  refused while only held slots are left. The `peer.inbound.held_seats`
  metrics count the slots that are held, taken back, and refused
  ([#2397](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2397)).
