## Changed

- When an outbound connection that was open for 10 minutes closes, Zakura now
  dials the peer again 10, 30, 60 and 120 seconds later, until one dial
  succeeds. This reconnects to a peer that restarted before other nodes take
  its free inbound slots. Each redial uses an outbound connection slot, and a
  banned peer or a peer that is already connected again is not redialed. The
  `peer.outbound.redials` metrics count the redials that are scheduled,
  attempted, and reconnected
  ([#2398](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2398)).
