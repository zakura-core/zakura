## Changed

- Zakura now keeps an inbound connection slot reserved for a peer that has
  just disconnected, so a node that restarts can connect to its peers again.
  A peer whose connection was open for 10 minutes keeps its slot for its IP
  address for 3 minutes after it disconnects. At most a tenth of the inbound
  connection limit is held this way, and new peers are refused while only
  held slots are left. The `peer.inbound.held_seats` metrics count the slots
  that are held, taken back, and refused
  ([#2397](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2397)).
