## Fixed

- A peer now gives its legacy block advert window entry back once its `inv` is written, for
  every block size, instead of holding that entry until it requests the block or
  `BLOCK_REQUEST_WAIT` (2 s) passes. A requested body above
  `advert_window_small_size_bytes`, or of unknown size, waits for one of the shared upload
  slots for at most 5 s before it is written, so a burst of requests cannot start every upload
  at once ([#2077](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2077)).
