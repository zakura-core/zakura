## Fixed

- Prevent old height claims from bypassing the Mainnet download work floor once
  the committed tip reaches its boundary, and require the floor for missing-parent
  recovery even during historical synchronization.
- Reject insufficient-work blocks before they can change sync download throttling
  ([#1927](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1927)).
- Validate downloaded targets and proof of work before scheduling, preserve peer
  scoring for invalid headers, and prevent stale tasks from changing a new sync
  round's throttling.
- Restart sync rounds that keep receiving rejected blocks without verified chain
  progress, and retry a failed attached parent once through the child's advertiser.
