## Changed

- On an equal-work tie, a block whose header arrived first over native header
  sync now keeps its place when its body follows within a deadline of 500 ms
  plus its size at 1 MiB per second, capped at 2 seconds. A full block
  therefore no longer loses to an empty block announced later. A body that
  arrives after the deadline is ranked by arrival, as before, and a node does
  not abandon a block it mined for a rival whose header came first. The
  `sync.block.announcement_claim` counter and the
  `sync.block.announcement_claim.age.seconds` histogram record the outcomes by
  block size, and `sync.block.announcement_claim.refused` counts claims refused
  by a full table
  ([#2605](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2605)).
- In the `zakura-consensus` crate, `router::init_with_read_state` takes the
  `AnnouncementClaims` table that header sync writes to
  ([#2605](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2605)).
