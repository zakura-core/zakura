## Fixed

- Mempool fee rejections and evictions now suppress every authorization
  variant with the same transaction effects until the next block. Changing
  signatures or proofs cannot bypass rejection tracking to trigger repeated
  downloading and verification. Smaller variants are also suppressed;
  verification and standardness failures still match exact authorizing data
  ([#1252](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1252)).
