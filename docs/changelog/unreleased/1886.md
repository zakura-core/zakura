## Changed

- Experimental: legacy block broadcasts can size how many peers they advertise a
  block to at once from the block's size and the node's measured upload
  bandwidth, so large blocks reach their first peers sooner. On Linux, Zakura
  measures upload bandwidth from the bytes peers acknowledge; until then, and on
  other platforms, broadcasts keep advertising to 32 peers at a time. New
  `[network]` settings `advert_target_secs`, `advert_window_initial`,
  `advert_window_min`, `advert_window_max`, and `advert_window_fixed` control
  it. A block broadcast no longer stops a broadcast of a higher block
  ([#1886](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1886)).
