## Rollback preservation

Each host retains `/root/nu7-v3-rollback` with actual running V2 executable backups,
configuration, service definitions, and inventory. The primary also retains the
old dashboard/faucet files, Caddy configuration, public manifest/config, and both
online and final V2 claims backups. Original V2 chain directories are untouched.

On a confirmed rollout failure, stop all V3 miners first and retain V3 evidence.
Restore V2 executables, configurations, state roots, faucet claims selection,
dashboard files, regional monitoring files/drop-ins, and publication as one
generation. Verify V2 validators agree before restarting its miners and faucet.
Never open V2 history under the V3 consensus configuration or publish mixed
V2/V3 metadata. The V3 snapshot URL remains immutable even after rollback.
