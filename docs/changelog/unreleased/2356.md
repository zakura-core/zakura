## Changed

- `getblocktemplate` no longer waits for the mempool on a new tip when the
  wait is not expected to pay off. The mempool now reports how much work a
  new block is before it removes that block's transactions, and a long poll
  answered on the new tip returns the coinbase-only template at once when
  the mempool held no transactions or reports more work than it is expected
  to finish within the 10 ms wait. Otherwise it waits as before
  ([#2356](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2356)).
