## Security

- Bound legacy P2P receive work before allocation. Impossible message sizes are rejected at the frame header, a request that stalls mid-read for 10 seconds closes its connection, responses stop at 8,192 frames on both ends, and decoded gossip takes at most 8 places in the queue it shares with requests, no more than 2 of them from one peer ([#1564](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1564)).
