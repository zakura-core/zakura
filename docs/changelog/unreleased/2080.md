## Changed

- Raised the default legacy block advert window from 64 peers at or below
  100 KiB and 32 at or above 1.5 MiB to 2000 and 1000. Peers that already
  have a block no longer delay its `inv` to other peers by 3 s per wave. Set
  `advert_window_min = 32` and `advert_window_max = 64` in `[network]` to keep
  the old fan-out
  ([#2080](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/2080)).
