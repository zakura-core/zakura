## Changed

- Experimental: legacy block broadcasts size how many peers they advertise a
  block to at once from the block's size, so a large block goes to fewer peers
  at a time. New `[network]` settings `advert_window_min` (default 32),
  `advert_window_max` (default 48), and `advert_window_full_size_bytes` (default
  1.5 MiB) control it
  ([#1886](https://github.com/zakura-core/zakura/pull/1886)).
